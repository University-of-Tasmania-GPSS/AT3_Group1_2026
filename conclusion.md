-	We isolated two separate study areas on the East and West of the river Derwent in Hobart, Tasmania.

-	We utilised building footprints to isolate our roof areas.

-	We successfully built a simplified solar radiation model using Python-based code.

-	We compared it with UMEP’s Solar Energy of Building Envelopes (SEBE) model in QGIS.

-	The Python model produced an optimistic estimate of roof irradiance, when compared with the SEBE analysis over the same areas.

**This study was a useful first step, exploring the power of Python-based code to run a custom-made analysis, that can be built upon, further refined and enhanced, depending on the area of investigation and the desired outcomes.**
